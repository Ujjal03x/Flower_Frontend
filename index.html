Our story

Small studio, big bunches.

Sweet Florist started as a single stall and a stack of buckets, built on one idea: flowers should look like they came from somewhere, not a warehouse. We still buy in person, still cut arrangements to order, and still turn work away during the weeks we can't do it properly.

Every bunch is put together by hand the morning it goes out, using whatever the growers we work with are proud of that week.
